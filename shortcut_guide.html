Step 15
Name it "WW Health" and tap Done
Tap the name field at the very top of the editor (shows "New Shortcut"). Type exactly WW Health — capital W, capital H, one space. Tap Done. Then tap ← back arrow to exit and save.

Step 17 · First Run
Complete a workout → tap LOG TO APPLE HEALTH → allow both prompts
Go to Workout Widget. Complete a workout and tap the red LOG TO APPLE HEALTH button on the completion screen. Two one-time permission dialogs will appear.
① Shortcut permission dialog:
🔗
"Workout Widget" wants to open "WW Health"
This will run the shortcut and log your workout.
Tap Always Allow — not "Allow Once". This prevents the prompt from reappearing every workout.
